# Patent Disclosure Draft — NOT A FILED PATENT

## Working title
**System and Method for Context-Versioned Multi-Agent Decision Orchestration with Policy-Governed Risk Validation and Controlled Execution**

## Field
The disclosure relates to computer-implemented systems for financial market data processing, decision-support, artificial intelligence orchestration, policy evaluation, and controlled execution.

## Technical problem
Existing systems may connect algorithmic or AI-generated recommendations to downstream actions without a sufficiently explicit lineage between source data, context, model state, decision, validation, policy state and execution. This creates difficulties in reproducibility, auditing, failure containment and controlled automation.

## Summary
The disclosed system receives data, constructs a versioned context, invokes one or more analysis agents, validates structured decisions, applies a policy/risk layer, and only then exposes an execution-capable action through a guarded interface. The system records the lineage required to reconstruct the decision.

## Detailed embodiment
1. Receive market and portfolio information.
2. Normalize and validate inputs.
3. Build a context artifact.
4. Attach version identifiers to context and configuration.
5. Invoke multiple analysis components.
6. Produce structured candidate decisions.
7. Validate structural and semantic constraints.
8. Apply policy and risk evaluation.
9. Require approval where configured.
10. Create a guarded execution request.
11. Submit through an adapter.
12. Monitor and reconcile actual state.
13. Store audit lineage.
14. Support later replay.
