## Change control
Every architecture change should record:
- change ID;
- author;
- timestamp;
- reason;
- affected nodes;
- old behavior;
- new behavior;
- tests;
- approval;
- artifact hash.

## Recommended preservation
Create WORM/immutable or otherwise access-controlled archives for milestone releases and keep independent backups.

## Disclosure control
Before public disclosure of a potentially patentable technical concept, consult patent counsel regarding filing strategy and applicable grace-period/novelty rules.
